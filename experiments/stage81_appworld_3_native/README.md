# Stage 8.1 — Three native AppWorld evaluations

## Scientific scope
Three authentic AppWorld development-task native evaluations and local tool logs. These runs produced no AURA certificates, and their outcomes must not be counted among the 59 AURA-certified episodes.

## Original evidence source
`AURA_APPWORLD_STAGE8_1_v7_RESULTS.zip`. The file exists byte-preserved beneath `archive/`; its extracted members appear under experiment-specific `raw/` or `prototype/` paths when applicable. Find exact paths and SHA256 digests in `evidence/master_source_registry.csv` and `evidence/master_archive_member_registry.csv`.

## How to inspect or reproduce
Review EXPERIMENT_SUMMARY.json, three track_B task directories and official_evaluation summaries. Historical failed v5/v6 setup notebooks are retained in archive/development_runs.

## Data labels and limitations
- An execution record is not the same as a test, benchmark calibration, planner world, or certificate replay.
- Keep FAIL, UNCERTAIN, skipped or interrupted cases rather than excluding them.
- Do not claim that all experiments were executed in one environment.
- Cite the archived original outputs, not handwritten summaries, in publication figures.
