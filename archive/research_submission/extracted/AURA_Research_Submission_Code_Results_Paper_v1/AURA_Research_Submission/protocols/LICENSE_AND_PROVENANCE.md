AppWorld task evaluations derive from the AppWorld benchmark (https://appworld.dev/); follow its authors' licensing and dataset redistribution restrictions before posting raw third-party benchmark data publicly. This locally shared research pack may contain externally sourced execution records; do not automatically upload the full ZIP to a public repository without reviewing those rights and any private information.

The Stage 7/CERA results are researcher-authored experiments; the finite-world cost units and timing measurements are not externally calibrated service pricing. Scripted faults and generated controls must remain clearly identified.

Hash verification establishes that the evidence bytes match the saved archive manifests; it cannot prove that a model actually executed on remote hardware or that recorded observations were independently attested.
